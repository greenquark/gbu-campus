# Environment asset sources

- `sky.hdr`: **Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky)**, 1K HDR, Greg Zaal (original) and Jarod Guest (sky edits), Poly Haven. CC0. Downloaded 2026-09-29 from the public asset API. Asset page: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky . File: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/kloppenheim_06_puresky_1k.hdr . License: https://polyhaven.com/license . Expected file size 1,173,154 bytes, published MD5 `995d68b1656f26452572645c0ffe898b`.
- `../campus-reference.glb`: copied from the existing `Photo_Model/GBU_Photo_Model.glb`, produced locally from the user's supplied photograph. The exterior is interpretive and unmeasured; rear elevations and roof are inferred. The inscription is substitute Kai lettering, not an extracted official logo.
- `paving-diff.jpg`, `paving-nor_gl.jpg`, `paving-rough.jpg`: **Floor Tiles 02**, 1K JPG color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/floor_tiles_02 . Used at a four-metre texture repeat with a subtle warm tint and restrained normal strength. Files obtained through https://api.polyhaven.com/files/floor_tiles_02 and the corresponding public download endpoint under `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/floor_tiles_02/`.
- Stone trim, timber, grass, leaf cutouts, lobby art, contact occlusion and sign textures are seeded local procedural textures in `src/materials.js` / `src/world.js`. No paid generation service or remote runtime fetch is used. The water uses one shared planar reflection render for both basins, plus subtle procedural ripple distortion.
- The entrance, concept lobby, furnishings, pool arrangement and planting were authored for this virtual visit; they are not claimed as a surveyed representation of the actual interior or landscape.
